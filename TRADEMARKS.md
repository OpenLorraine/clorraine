# Clorraine Trademark Policy

The names "Clorraine" and "OpenLorraine", and the Clorraine logo, are
trademarks of OpenLorraine. This policy explains their use. It is not a license
to use the marks.

## Allowed without permission

- Refer to Clorraine by name in reviews, tutorials, courses, documentation,
  community discussion, and comparisons.
- State accurately that your software or hardware is compatible with, uses, or
  is based on Clorraine.
- Redistribute unmodified official releases under the Apache License 2.0.
- Describe a fork factually as "a fork of Clorraine", provided the fork has a
  different name and logo and does not imply endorsement.

## Requires written permission

- Using "Clorraine" or the logo as the name or branding of a modified
  compiler, product, service, or distribution.
- Using the logo in a way that suggests official status, sponsorship, or
  endorsement.
- Registering a domain, social account, or company name that is confusingly
  similar to Clorraine or OpenLorraine.

## Forks

Forks are welcome under the Apache License 2.0. A fork must use a different
name and logo and must not present itself as the official Clorraine project.
It may state factually that it is based on Clorraine.
