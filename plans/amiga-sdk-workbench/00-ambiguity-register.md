## Scope baseline

Bundle 8 (`8-workbench`) from `docs/amiga-sdk/registry.tsv`: AppIcon/AppWindow
and messages (should), icon library (should), commodity broker (could),
locale.library (could, 2.1), ARexx message port (could), clipboard device
(should), serial/parallel/printer (could), expansion/keymap/card/nonvolatile
(could), and the Workbench integration demo (should, 3.1). No language,
grammar, diagnostic, or ABI change.
